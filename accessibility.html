Accessibility

Access for everyone

Vinnik Net is committed to welcoming all guests. Entrance, seating and restrooms are designed for accessibility where possible.

  • Wheelchair-friendly entrance — ask staff to meet you at the side door if needed.
  • Service animals welcome.
  • Large-print menus and forms available on request.
  • Contact support@vinniknet.com before your visit for specific needs.
