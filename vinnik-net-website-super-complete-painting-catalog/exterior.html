Exterior coating starts with surface condition.

What the scope should define

  • Surfaces and approximate quantities
  • Current condition and repair assumptions
  • Protection and access requirements
  • Primer / finish assumptions
  • Color and sheen selections
  • Cleanup and handoff expectations
