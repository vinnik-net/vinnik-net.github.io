FAQ

Questions worth answering before paint day.

How is painting priced?+

Final pricing usually depends on surfaces, condition, prep, access, materials, color changes and schedule. The catalog uses demo starting prices only.

Do you patch holes before painting?+

Minor and larger repairs should be identified during scope review. The final estimate should say exactly which repairs are included.

How many coats are included?+

That depends on the specified coating system, color transition and substrate. The final written scope should define the coating assumptions.

Do you move furniture?+

The real business should define customer versus crew responsibilities for furniture, wall décor and fragile items.

What paint do you use?+

The final contractor should publish approved product lines and sheen recommendations rather than making a generic promise.

Do you paint occupied homes?+

The workflow can support occupied projects, but access, pets, room sequencing and ventilation need to be planned.

What about lead paint?+

Pre-1978 surfaces may require lead-safe assessment and regulated work practices. Verify the contractor’s actual certifications and local requirements.

Can you work around business hours?+

Commercial scheduling can be phased around operations when the final scope and staffing allow it.
